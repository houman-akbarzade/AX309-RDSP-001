# AX309-RDSP-001 — S02 limited revision v0.8 -> v0.9
Only C-CFAR-02 statistical qualification and corresponding REQ-CFAR-02 matrix fields have changed.
1. Separates NOMINAL 95% coverage target from MINIMUM ACCEPTABLE 93% conditional coverage floor. At true 95% coverage the former v0.8 Wilson lower >=95% rule has only ~5% power, and with 15 independent conditions has negligible joint power.
2. Increases qualification repetitions per condition 1000 -> 3000 for 15 conditions to secure high joint qualification probability at target 95% coverage; no original nested bootstrap restored.
3. Reports conditional coverage among VALID intervals, unconditional VALID-AND-COVER fraction among ALL studies (inconclusive cannot count as successful) and independent INCONCLUSIVE rate.
4. Predeclares one-sided Wilson lower conditional >=93%, lower unconditional >=90%, upper inconclusive <=5% for all 15 conditions; hard trial count and freeze seeds. Detailed exact-binomial power table includes dependence on true conditional coverage and true inconclusive rate.
5. Beta-binomial count-only scenarios remain stress sensitivity, not realistic spatial CA-CFAR coverage proof; S03 real radar-chain spatial diagnostics remain mandatory BEFORE main Pfa acceptance, with blocking process for failures.
6. The rest of S02 v0.8 (C06, signal, UART, Doppler, association, resource and Gate restrictions) is copied unchanged. C04 still open, C08 still open. No S03 or simulations executed.
