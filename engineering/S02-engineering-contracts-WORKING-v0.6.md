Gate-S02 OPEN. Six proposed deterministic contracts for engineer review, not an approved baseline; no S03/RTL/FPGA execution.

## C-SIG-01 | Discrete LFM and matched filter
N=128, fs=1e6 Hz, L=16 samples, pulse length T=16us, B=250kHz; midpoint sample t_j=(j+0.5)/fs for j=0..15 and chirp rate mu=B/T. Complex baseband pulse s[j]=exp(i*2*pi*(-B*t_j/2+mu*t_j*t_j/2)). Its continuous instantaneous frequency sweeps -125 to +125kHz; sampled first/last instantaneous frequencies are -117187.5 and +117187.5Hz. Rectangular duration 16 samples and s=0 outside support. Reference chirp unit modulus, deterministic phase at t=0=0.
Input echo for integer delay k and slow pulse m: a*s[n-k]*exp(i*2*pi*fd*m*PRI+i*phi), PRI=1ms, fd=2*v/lambda with positive v approaching. Each echo lies k..k+15; full echo contained iff 0<=k<=112. Physical range per delay sample uses exact c=299792458m/s, deltaR=149.896229m; 150m is rounding only. Grid suite target delays k=20,40,60 represent close approximations to 3,6,9km; exact nominal distances use a fractional-delay separately defined suite, not silently rounded truth.
h[j]=conj(s[15-j]) for j=0..15. Linear convolution z=x*h has output index q=0..142. Delay-aligned MF p[k]=z[k+15], with zeros outside input support. Noiseless isolated full echo has peak index exactly k after delay compensation, and theoretical peak magnitude=16*|a|. Define MF range search k=0..112; edge k=0,112 examined separately. Do not mistake full-echo support for CFAR CUT-valid range. Peak equality cases are explicit errors in clean single-target grid tests, not silently resolved.
Numerical taps and fixed-point bit growth follow S04; measured range sidelobes and two-target separability are S03 responsibilities. 600m nominal c/(2B) resolution is theory, not measured outcome.

## C-DAT-01 | Binary IQ, noise, SCR and reproducibility
IQ wire bytes: pulse-major m=0..32 then range n=0..127; each sample I then Q, little-endian two's-complement int16 each, total 33*128*4=16896B. Candidate signed Q1.15 binary conversion for normalized signal: integer=clip(round_to_nearest_ties_even(x*32768),-32768,32767). Exact non-clipping source normalization to be tracked; any saturated samples reported and clean vectors require zero saturations. Raw byte vector and metadata have separate SHA-256 hashes.
Save fixed PCG64 algorithm/version with NumPy version, 128-bit seed decimal representation and independent CPI/scenario substream seeds; generator source hash, environment lock, sample ordering, exact parameter manifest, quantizer mode, clipping count and raw-vector hash. Bit-identical replay required for pinned environment and frozen vector artifact; not claimed across unrelated library versions.
For isolated target i, average P_echo_i=sum_j|a_i*s[j]|²/16 over active echo only; w~CN(0,sigma²), E|w|²=sigma², real/imag each variance sigma²/2. SNR_i=10log10(P_echo_i/sigma²) measured at ideal complex input before summing noise and before quantization. At 0,10,20dB choose sigma² accordingly; finite record measured SNR and Monte Carlo confidence reported separately without claiming exact realized noise power each CPI.
SCR_i=10log10(P_echo_i/P_clutter_i); P_clutter_i is average |c[m,n]|² on the active echo support n=k_i..k_i+15, m=0..32. For multi-target cases report each mask; the +10/-10dB scenario uses homogeneous clutter power across all defined masks; heterogeneous clutter reports distinct SCRs. No-clutter separate. Define statistical sample tolerance in S03 generator test before execution. Input echoes fractional delay off grid belong to separate explicit suite.

## C-DOP-01 | MTI, FFT, scoring association
MTI baseline first difference y[m,k]=p[m+1,k]-p[m,k], m=0..31 (33 into 32 pulses). Theoretical transfer H(fd)=exp(i*2pi*fd*PRI)-1 for this defined subtraction; zero at fd=0. Complex FFT Y[k,b]=sum_{m=0}^{31}y[m,k]*exp(-i*2pi*b*m/32), b=0..31, rectangular window. Signed bin b_s=b for 0..15, b_s=b-32 for 16..31 (bin 16 = negative Nyquist). v_b=b_s*c*PRF/(2*fc*32), fc=10GHz, PRF=1000; nominal bin delta v=0.468425715625m/s with exact c; historical half-bin acceptance 0.234375m/s is based on rounded c=3e8. For compatibility of previously approved threshold, use nominal conversion c_ref=3e8m/s for Doppler/radar test labels and separate exact-c supplementary results; do not mix conventions in one test. With c_ref, bin=0.46875m/s and half-bin=0.234375m/s. Positive approaching uses positive signed bin. No velocity unwrapping required.
Dominant identifiable peak: strictly positive power and unique bin maximum strictly greater than both circular adjacent bin powers; tie uses smallest signed bin and boundary scenarios reported separately, not silently included in half-bin guarantee. Peak leakage measure in dB: 10log10(sum power outside peak / peak power), evaluated on target-only isolated FFT and with/without MTI. A completely stationary target is nulled and not eligible for peak localization.
Truth matching is *host scoring*, not FPGA clustering: feasible pair iff |dR|<=2 range samples and |dv|<=1 Doppler bin; normalized C=(dR/(2*deltaR))²+(dv/deltaV)². Maximize cardinality first, then minimize sum C, then resolve exact equal-optimum ties lexicographically by ordered (truth ID,detection ID) pairs. Every detection and truth used at most once. Unmatched detections false, unmatched truths missed. Scoring gate of two samples is not a looser acceptance tolerance for hardware range estimation.

## C-CFAR-01 | 2D CA-CFAR geometry and statistics
Input linear power map 128x32 after MTI and FFT. CA-CFAR window 7x7: one guard cell per axis on each side and two outer training layers, therefore 49 total positions minus central 3x3 = 40 training cells. Baseline use no Doppler wrapping, no edge padding; complete-echo valid range k=0..112. CUT valid k=3..109, b=3..28; 107*26=2782 valid CUTs per CPI. Training mean Z=1/40 sum(training linear powers). Detect only if P_CUT > alpha*Z (strict >). Out-of-domain CUTs marked invalid. Input 3/6/9km targets at k20,40,60 all interior.
Analytic IID-exponential initialization alpha=40*(0.001**(-1/40)-1) approx 7.54, NOT a guaranteed Pfa after matched filtering, MTI, FFT and window correlations. A disjoint independent calibration set may calibrate alpha by a predeclared monotonic bisection to approach design target; once frozen, alpha cannot be tuned on validation data.
Pre-register noise-only Gaussian validation with 4096 independently seeded CPIs (no targets, no clutter), exactly 2782 eligible CUT per CPI if numeric validity passes. No optional early stopping or after-result extensions. Report p_hat=(sum_i false_alarms_i)/(4096*2782), together with per-CPI alarm counts. The independent statistical cluster is the CPI, not a CUT.
One-sided 95% upper confidence bound: generate 10000 nonparametric bootstrap resamples of 4096 whole CPI alarm counts, seed 20261109, compute pooled fractions and take nearest-rank 9500 among sorted bootstrap fractions. PASS if upper <=1.5e-3, but only after **method coverage qualification**: a separately frozen synthetic correlated-count study at p around 1e-3 with known generating probability must show >=95% one-sided coverage (with statistical uncertainty specified in its protocol before running). If method qualification fails, label statistical result INCONCLUSIVE and return to engineer for pre-approved revision rather than selecting a better CI post-hoc. This method is a proposed test protocol, not a claim of validated coverage.
Pd goal>=0.9 at 20dB SNR, one target/no clutter, is a design target pending S03 reference feasibility. At 0/10dB report Pd only. Clutter, correlated/heterogeneous distributions have their own separate tests without extrapolating noise-only Pfa. Exact per-CUT threshold rounding and bit width follow S04.

## C-UART-01 | FPGA UART transport and debug readback
Onboard CP2102GM, initial UART 8N1 at 115200 baud (must be confirmed on board). Byte frame: four-byte SOF A5 5A C3 3C; uint8 version=1; uint8 type; uint8 flags; little-endian uint32 sequence; little-endian uint16 payload length 0..512; payload; little-endian uint32 CRC-32. CRC over bytes from version through end of payload, excluding SOF and CRC itself. CRC-32/ISO-HDLC reflected polynomial 0xEDB88320, initial register FFFFFFFF, final XOR FFFFFFFF; standard check ASCII 123456789 -> CBF43926. Maximum frame size 529 bytes. Parser accepts frame only after version/type/length/expected sequence and CRC validation, and resynchronizes by byte scanning after malformed length, timeout or CRC error. SOF appearing in payload alone is not sufficient for acceptance.
Define packet TYPE command, IQ_UPLOAD, STAGE_REQUEST, STAGE_DATA, RESULT, ACK, NACK, STATUS; distinct values fixed by ICD no later than S05. IQ_UPLOAD carries uint32 CPI ID and uint32 byte offset preceding chunk data. Reassembly must cover all 16896 bytes with no missing or overlapping byte ranges; full CPI uploaded and acknowledged before run command. A readback or full-CPI digest must be used to verify host bytes; SHA-256 on FPGA is not required if equivalent byte-accurate host readback exists.
Stop-and-wait ARQ: monotonic sequence per link direction; ACK exact sequence, duplicate acknowledged idempotently without reapplying payload, out-of-order NACK with expected sequence, up to 3 retransmissions after initial send, timeout nominal 2s per frame at configured 115200 baud, abort CPI with explicit status on exhaustion; no invisible completion. Fault test matrix corruption, truncation, CRC changes, duplicate, missing, reorder, timeout and recovery. For all *injected test faults*, require either appropriate retry/recovery or unambiguous failure, never a falsely successful CPI. CRC detects many but not mathematically all error patterns.
Full intermediate output for each of seven FPGA functions accessible by stage request and segmented STAGE_DATA with CPI/offset/type/schema; deterministic reruns allowed without simultaneous buffer retention. Binary readback compared bytewise with corresponding bit-true reference under identical initial conditions. Timing metrics distinguish link transfer time and FPGA-only cycle measurement.

## C-TRACE-01 | Acceptance matrix and deferred ownership
Every requirement has stable ID, binding/design-target/deferred class, quantity and unit, stimulus and test domain, reference method, predeclared pass/fail oracle, evidence artifact and hash, owner and due stage, and recorded status. Seven DSP processing functions on AX309 FPGA remain binding but seven separate RTL modules are not required. No resource overuse or failing STA allowed; 90% uniform margin was explicitly NOT approved.
Pre-Gate S02: engineer must review and approve discrete signal and UART contracts, CFAR geometry and statistically qualified plan, standardized data and matching policy, traceability and accepted downstream deferral conditions. Any proposed metric based on S03 results cannot silently be promoted to mandatory target before the engineer has approved the decision criterion.
S03 owns: actual functional reference, measured valid MF behavior, range resolution and noisy single-target statistics, statistical CFAR and Pd feasibility, Doppler leakage and independent CI-method coverage qualification. S04 owns arithmetic word lengths, rounding, saturation/overflow and floating-vs-bittrue error budgets while exact FPGA-bittrue matching is binding. S05 owns BRAM/SDRAM selection, simultaneous memory ports, buffer lifetimes, per-resource headroom, selected RTL organization, target list capacity/overflow prioritization, counter sizing and link timing feasibility. S08/S09 own resource mapping and STA evidence. S11 owns board UART throughput, integrity and physical radar-chain test evidence.
Exit of S02 approves the measurable *method*, not a fabricated FPGA test result or numerical hardware performance.
