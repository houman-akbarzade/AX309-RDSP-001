# AX309-RDSP-001 — S01 Problem Definition, Draft v0.2 (ENGINEER REVIEW)

Status: S01 Gate OPEN; draft submitted for review; no S02 authorization.
Authoritative basis: engineer messages referenced in decisions/S01-decisions.json and AX309 Manual Rev 1.0 (2019-04-26), verified via connected Drive PDF (evidence/source-index.json). No synthesis, STA or physical board results.

## Problem and deliverable
Demonstrate real execution on ALINX AX309 (Spartan-6 XC6SLX9-2FTG256C) of the entire eight-stage range/Doppler detection chain, with Python deterministic input generation and stagewise software reference: (1) multi-target IQ, thermal noise, clutter; (2) 16-us 250-kHz LFM matched filter; (3) CPI range matrix; (4) MTI; (5) 32-point Doppler FFT/range-Doppler matrix; (6) 2D CFAR; (7) detection and clustering; (8) target list of range, signed radial velocity and signal power. Hardware may run sequentially/time-multiplexed without real-time deadline. Individual block tests and full-chain tests mandatory. Results, intermediate outputs and timing counters read back through USB-UART, with IQ uploaded the same way. Host transfer latency and board-only processing latency shall be measured and reported separately. Architectural interchangeability for future external ADC/SDR is desirable.

## Approved time and signal conventions
Input IQ complex signed 16+16-bit (input representation candidate, full internal widths to be designed), sample rate 1 MS/s, 128 samples/pulse, PRI 1 ms, 33 pulses/CPI as candidate to support first-order pulse-to-pulse MTI and 32-point Doppler FFT. Input receive sample n=0 means transmitted LFM start t=0, and echo delay 2R/c. fc=10 GHz is only an analytic radar parameter, NOT onboard RF. Positive radial velocity means approaching. A consistent phase convention (e.g. +2*pi*fd*m*PRI for approaching and fd=2v/lambda) must be tested against output bin indexing; not yet verified. N=128 corresponds to time window 128 us and nominal 19.2-km span, not guaranteed uncorrupted detection span. Correct range origin after linear matched filtering and edge effects must be documented.

## Approved experimental scenario
Targets at R=[3,6,9] km and v=[-3,+1.5,+5] m/s respectively, with equal *received* echo powers for the initial base case; investigate unequal received powers and optionally geometric attenuation later. SNR before matched filter at 0/10/20 dB; baseline at 20 dB. Formal definition of power averaged over target echo support, noise CN(0,sigma^2) convention (E|n|^2=sigma^2; each Cartesian real component variance sigma^2/2), and actual measuring reference must be ratified in S02. Clutter: zero-clutter baseline, simple stationary clutter with slow-time constant phase, strong stationary clutter MTI test, weak/strong power sweeps relative to received target power, and later wider time-correlated clutter. Numerical SCR values in S02. Separate stationary target test for MTI DC notch and near-window-edge detection tests. Synthetic data must be reproducible with saved seed and generator version.

## Preliminary sizing and architectural feasibility
One CPI raw IQ: 33*128*4=16,896 bytes. A 128x32 complex 16+16-bit range/Doppler matrix: 16,384 bytes. A 128x32 unsigned 32-bit power map: 16,384 bytes. Summing these three ONLY: 49,664 bytes (<72 KiB nominal BRAM), but leaves only 24,064 bytes before BRAM banking, 18Kb/9Kb primitive granularity, filter intermediate widths, MTI staging, FFT twiddles, line buffers, CFAR, control and debug. Need actual BRAM budget and arbitration proof; cannot assume all three reside simultaneously, or SDRAM is required. External 32 MiB SDRAM is more than capacity needs, but its 16-bit controller, timings and FPGA pin use introduce design cost and schedule risk. Time-multiplexing 16 DSP blocks may make stages computationally feasible at 50 MHz because batch mode is allowed; this is NOT a verified utilization/throughput guarantee. FFT, 2D CFAR and clustering architecture remain open and may require stream/tile passes and external or reusable memories.
UART theoretical 8N1 transfer of 16,896 bytes: ~1.467 s at 115200 baud; ~0.183 s at 921600 baud, excluding framing/retries; device capabilities and actual end-to-end rates are unverified. 32x128 power map alone is 16,384 bytes; several intermediate maps may dominate transfer time, not compute time. CP2102GM protocol and serial baud/host flow control must be tested before finalized.
Pulse 16 us at 1 MS/s -> 16 samples, BT=4, nominal c/(2B) ~600 m. With 32-point Doppler FFT and PRF 1 kHz, bin spacing 31.25 Hz and nominal unambiguous v range ±7.5 m/s for 10 GHz (assuming centered Doppler); MTI output from 33 pulses may be 32 pulses for first-difference implementation. This is a candidate and not approved final implementation.

## Feasibility risks (NOT closure results)
R1 HIGH: simultaneous memory use and control complexity on XC6SLX9, including memory banking and SDRAM controller.
R2 HIGH: complete on-FPGA 2D CFAR + clustering + output formatting on small logic/BRAM budget; stage-by-stage architecture, streaming and time reuse may be required.
R3 MEDIUM: unsupported timing/baud assumptions for CP2102GM, plus long debug-map readback; no real-time host transfer required.
R4 MEDIUM: pulse BT=4, finite MF support and boundary effects; theoretical 600 m is not equivalent to achieved two-target separability.
R5 MEDIUM: MTI removes stationary target and attenuates slow movers, and range/Doppler sign consistency must be tested.
R6 MEDIUM: Python floating-point reference vs fixed-point FPGA quantization; thresholds and tolerances need S02/S04 budgets.
R7 HIGH: feasibility cannot be proven at S01 without specific RTL/IP, ISE 14.7 synthesis and place-route; a negative result may force S05 architectural revision or explicit requirement renegotiation.
